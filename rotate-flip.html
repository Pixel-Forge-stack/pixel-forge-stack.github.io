What It Does

Rotate and Flip Images

The tool rotates an image 90° left, 90° right or 180°, and flips it horizontally (mirror) or vertically. You can combine actions and download the result as JPG, PNG or WebP.

Rotating turns the picture; flipping creates a mirror image. A sideways photo needs a rotation, not a flip.
Why Photos Appear Sideways

Orientation and Camera Data

Many cameras and phones save a photo in one orientation and store a note in the file telling apps how to turn it. Some software ignores that note, so the same photo can look upright in one place and sideways in another. Rotating and saving a new file makes the orientation part of the pixels.

Check the preview before downloading, since the correct direction depends on how the photo was taken. The saved file is a new image made from the pixels, so details such as EXIF data are normally not carried over.

Quality

Saving the Result

A rotation by 90° steps does not change pixel values, but saving as JPG or WebP encodes the picture again, which is lossy. Choose PNG to avoid that, at the cost of a larger file. The tool handles one image at a time and the image is not uploaded to our servers.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my photo sideways? ▾
The file probably stores an orientation note that the app or site you are using does not apply. Rotate it here and save a new copy.
Which should I use, rotate or flip? ▾
Rotate fixes sideways or upside-down photos. Flip mirrors the picture, for example to reverse text direction or match a layout.
Does rotating reduce quality? ▾
Not in the rotation itself, but saving as JPG or WebP applies compression. PNG avoids this.
Can I rotate by any angle? ▾
No. The tool supports 90°, 180° and 270°.
