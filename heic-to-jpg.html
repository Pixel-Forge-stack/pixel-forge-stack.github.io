What Is HEIC

HEIC and JPG: What Changes in the Conversion

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is a file format based on the HEIF standard. iPhones and iPads have used it as the default camera format since iOS 11. It generally stores a photo at a smaller file size than JPG for comparable visual quality, and it can hold extra data such as depth information.

The drawback is compatibility. Some Windows computers, Android devices, websites and older apps cannot open HEIC. JPG is one of the most widely supported image formats across devices, browsers, applications and websites, so converting is a common way to share, upload or print an iPhone photo.

What Changes

Quality, Size and Metadata

Quality. Converting to JPG is a lossy step. At 92% quality the difference is small for most photos, but fine detail can be slightly softer, especially after repeated edits and re-saves.File size. A JPG made from a HEIC photo is often larger than the original HEIC, because JPG is a less efficient format. If you need a smaller file, use Compress Image or Resize Image afterwards.Metadata. The new JPG is created from the pixels on a canvas, so details such as EXIF data and location are normally not carried over. Check the result if you rely on that information.Transparency. Photos from an iPhone camera do not normally have transparency, so this is rarely a concern for HEIC.
Good To Know

Changing the Format at the Source

On an iPhone you can change the camera format under Settings, Camera, Formats, by choosing Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPG, while existing photos stay HEIC. Many sharing options in iOS can also convert photos when you send them, though behaviour varies by app and settings.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the tool say it could not read my HEIC file? ▾
The tool relies on your browser to open the file, and not every browser or operating system can decode HEIC. Safari on an iPhone or Mac generally can. If yours cannot, try Safari on an Apple device, or convert the photo on the iPhone itself, for example by switching the camera format as described below.
Does converting HEIC to JPG reduce quality? ▾
Yes, slightly. JPG is a lossy format, so the image is re-encoded. The tool saves JPG at 92% quality, which keeps most photos looking very close to the original, but it is not an exact copy. Keep the original HEIC if you may need it later.
Is location and camera data kept in the JPG? ▾
Do not count on it. The tool redraws the image onto a canvas and saves a new JPG, which typically does not carry over metadata such as EXIF details or location. That can be useful for privacy, but it also means information like the capture date may be missing.
Why can I not open HEIC files on my Windows PC? ▾
Windows may need extra codecs, such as the HEIF Image Extensions from the Microsoft Store, and some HEIC photos also need HEVC support. Converting to JPG avoids the problem for files you want to share or upload.
How do I make my iPhone save JPG instead of HEIC? ▾
Open Settings, go to Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPG. Photos you already took stay in HEIC.
Can I convert several HEIC files at once? ▾
No. The tool converts one image per run. Choose the next file after downloading the first.
