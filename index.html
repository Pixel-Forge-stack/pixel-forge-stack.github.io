About the Tools

Free Image Tools That Work Right in Your Browser

Pixel Forge Stack is a set of free online image tools. You can resize an image, compress it to a smaller file size, crop it, rotate it, or convert it to another format. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Open a tool, choose your photo, and download the result.

Your images are not uploaded to any server. All the work happens inside your own browser, on your own device. This keeps your photos private and also makes the tools fast, because there is no waiting for a file to travel over the internet.

Many people come here for a very specific need. Some want to compress an image to 100 KB for an online form. Others need a passport size photo, a YouTube thumbnail, or a WhatsApp profile picture. We built a separate page with the right settings for each of these, so you do not have to guess the numbers.

If you are not sure which file format to pick, read our guide on JPG vs PNG vs WebP. If your resized photo looks soft, our article on why resized images look blurry explains what is going on and how to avoid it.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these image tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, as many times as you like. There is no signup, no watermark, and no paid plan.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Your images are processed inside your browser and never leave your device. We do not store or see your files.

Which file formats can I use?

Most tools accept JPG, PNG, and WEBP. Some tools also handle GIF, BMP, HEIC, and ICO files. Each tool page lists the formats it supports.

How do I make an image smaller in KB?

Use the Compress Image tool, or pick a ready made page such as compress to 100 KB or compress to 200 KB.

Will resizing or compressing reduce my image quality?

Making an image smaller usually keeps it looking sharp. Making it much larger than the original can make it look soft. Compression at a moderate quality setting keeps most of the detail while cutting the file size.

Do the tools work on a phone?

Yes. The tools work on Android, iPhone, tablets, and computers in any modern browser.
