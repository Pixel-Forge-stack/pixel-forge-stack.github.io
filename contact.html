Before You Email

Quick Answers

Is my image sent to you when I use a tool? ▾
No. Every tool on Pixel Forge Stack processes your image locally in your browser — it's never uploaded anywhere, including to us. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How fast will I get a reply?
We aim to respond within a few business days. Privacy-related requests are prioritized.
Can you fix a bug I found in a tool?
Yes — please include the tool name, your browser and device, and the steps to reproduce the issue. That helps us fix it faster.
