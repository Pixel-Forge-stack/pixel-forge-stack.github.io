Questions about the Privacy Policy are welcome. For anything personal, use email instead of a public issue.

Before You Write

Quick Answers

Is my image sent to you when I use a tool? ▾
No. The tools process your selected image locally in your browser, and the file is not uploaded to our image-processing servers. The site does use analytics and some third-party resources, which are described in our Privacy Policy.
Will I get a reply?
There is no guaranteed response time. This is a small, independently run project, and replies depend on available time.
Can you fix a bug I found in a tool?
Bug reports are welcome. Please include the tool name, your browser and device, and the steps to reproduce the issue.
