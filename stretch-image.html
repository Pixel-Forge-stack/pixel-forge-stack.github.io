What It Does

Stretching an Image to Exact Dimensions

Stretching sets a new width and height independently, so the picture is scaled by different amounts horizontally and vertically. Unlike normal resizing, it does not keep the aspect ratio, which means shapes become wider, taller or squashed.

It is useful when a fixed frame needs a picture that must fill it completely, or for deliberate effects. If you want a natural look, use Resize Image to keep the proportions, Crop Image to remove part of the picture, or Change Aspect Ratio to add background around it.

How It Works

Using the Tool

Choose an image, enter a width and height in pixels, pick JPG, PNG or WebP and download. The tool draws the entire image into the new size, with no cropping. Stretching by a large factor makes distortion obvious, and enlarging beyond the original size also softens the picture.

The tool processes one image at a time, and the image is not uploaded to our servers.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is stretching the same as resizing? ▾
No. Resizing normally keeps the aspect ratio; stretching deliberately changes it.
Why does my stretched image look blurry? ▾
If either dimension is larger than the original, the picture is enlarged, which cannot add detail.
Can I stretch only the width? ▾
Yes. Enter a new width and leave the height at the original value.
