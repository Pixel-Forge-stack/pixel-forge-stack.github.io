About This Size

What Is 1920 × 1080?

1920 × 1080 is known as Full HD or 1080p. It is a 16:9 frame with 2,073,600 pixels (about 2.07 megapixels) and a very common resolution for monitors, laptop screens, TVs and video.

People most often resize to 1920 × 1080 for desktop wallpapers, slide backgrounds, video overlays and screenshots that have to match a Full HD screen.

How the Tool Works

How This Preset Is Applied

The preset sets the output to exactly 1920 × 1080 pixels. It does not crop or add padding: the whole image is scaled into that frame. If your picture does not already have the same proportions (16:9), it will look stretched or squeezed, so crop it first.

Choose JPG or WebP for photographs and PNG when you need transparency or very sharp edges. The quality slider affects JPG and WebP only. WebP output depends on your browser, and the tool processes one image at a time. For general guidance on pixels, percentages, aspect ratio and upscaling, see Resize Image. To meet a file-size limit, follow up with Compress Image.

Practical Tips

Before You Resize

Crop to 16:9 first. Phone and camera photos are often 4:3 or 3:2, and a portrait photo is far from 16:9. The preset forces the exact frame, so non-16:9 photos get stretched. Crop with Crop Image or use Change Aspect Ratio first.

Use a large enough source. A small image enlarged to 1920 × 1080 will look soft, because new pixels cannot add real detail.

Mind the file size. A Full HD photo as PNG can be several megabytes. JPG or WebP at a moderate quality is usually much smaller; see Compress Image if you need a specific size.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1920 × 1080 the same as 1080p? ▾
1080p refers to 1080 horizontal lines of resolution. In practice it means the 1920 × 1080 Full HD frame.
Why does my photo look stretched at 1920 × 1080? ▾
The photo probably is not 16:9. The preset sets the exact size without cropping, so a different ratio gets squeezed or stretched. Crop to 16:9 first and then resize.
What size should I use for a YouTube thumbnail? ▾
Thumbnails are commonly made at 1280 × 720, which is also 16:9. See the YouTube Thumbnail resizer, and check YouTube's current guidance.
