1Overview

The short version: The image you select is processed locally in your browser. The image file is not uploaded to our image-processing servers. Like most websites, the site does load analytics and some third-party resources, which are described below.

Pixel Forge Stack is a collection of image tools that run in your web browser. This policy explains what happens to your images, what technical and usage data may be collected when you visit, and which third-party services are involved.

This policy describes how the site works at the time of the last update. It is not legal advice, and it may not cover every situation in every country.

2Image Data and Processing

When you select an image in one of the tools, the browser reads the file into memory and the tool processes it with the HTML5 Canvas API on your own device. The result is created in your browser and saved to your device when you click download.

The tool code does not send your image file to our image-processing servers. The tools do not require an account, and the site has no feature for storing images.

A few points are worth stating precisely:

  • Processing an image locally is different from the site making no network requests at all. Loading the page, fonts, scripts and analytics involves network requests (see sections 3, 4 and 6).
  • The tool code does not intentionally pass image content to analytics.
  • Files you download are saved on your device. What happens to them afterwards depends on your device and the apps you use.
  • Browser extensions or other software on your device can access web pages. This is outside our control.

3Usage Data and Analytics

The site uses Google Tag Manager to load analytics. Analytics services typically receive technical information that your browser sends with every request, and may record things such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on them.
  • Browser type, operating system, device type and screen size.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • How you arrived at the site (for example, a search engine or another website).
  • General interaction data, such as which page or tool you opened.

The exact data collected depends on how the Tag Manager container is configured, which can change over time.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

4Cookies and Local Storage

The tool pages do not use cookies or local storage for their own functionality. Cookies may be set by third-party services that the site loads, in particular Google Tag Manager and any analytics tags it activates. Which cookies are set depends on that configuration.

At the time of this update the site does not show a cookie consent banner. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking them may change how analytics behave without affecting the image tools.

5Advertising

The code in the site's repository does not currently include advertising scripts. If advertising is added in the future, this section will be updated to describe the networks involved and how cookies are used.

Google Tag Manager can load additional tags that are managed outside the site's source files, so this statement refers only to the site's own code.

6Third-Party Services

The site relies on a small number of external services. Your browser makes requests to them when a page loads, and each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google Tag Manager: loads analytics tags on all pages.
  • Google Fonts: serves the Inter typeface. Your browser requests the font files from Google.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): the Image to PDF page loads the jsPDF library from this CDN. The library runs in your browser, and your image is not sent to the CDN.

7Children's Privacy

Pixel Forge Stack is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete that information.

If you are under 18 years of age, please use this website only with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

8Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. These may include:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request that we correct any inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request that we delete your personal data.
  • The right to object to or restrict our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability.

The site has no user accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names or email addresses through the tools. The exception is what you choose to send us by email (see section 10). Data held by analytics, hosting and font providers is controlled by those providers, so some requests may need to be directed to them.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the tools we offer, or applicable laws and regulations. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. If we make significant changes, we may also provide a more prominent notice on our website.

Your continued use of Pixel Forge Stack after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10Contact Us

For questions about this policy, email thereputationcare@gmail.com. You can also open an issue on the project's public GitHub issue tracker, but issues are public, so please do not include personal details or images there.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to read and respond to your message. Email is also handled by your email provider and by Gmail, which hosts this mailbox, under their own privacy policies. We have not set a fixed retention period for emails.
