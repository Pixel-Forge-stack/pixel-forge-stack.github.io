1. Introduction

Welcome to Audio Toolkit ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and using our tools.

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • What information we collect and why
  • How we use and protect your information
  • Your rights regarding your data
  • How to contact us with privacy questions

By using Audio Toolkit, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

Key Principle: We believe your audio files and personal data belong to you. We don't sell, share, or exploit your information for profit.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Audio Files You Upload

When you use Audio Toolkit, you may upload audio files for processing. Here's what happens:

  • Processed Locally: Your audio file is loaded entirely into your browser's memory
  • Never Uploaded: Your file never leaves your device or reaches our servers
  • No Storage: Files are deleted from memory after processing. We have no access to them
  • Immediate Deletion: If you refresh your browser or close the page, the file is immediately cleared
Your Device → Browser Memory → Processing → Download Result
❌ Not sent to servers ❌ Not stored ❌ Not accessed by us

2.2 Server Logs & Analytics

We collect minimal technical information for website operation:

  • Server Logs: IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps (automatically logged by hosting provider)
  • Purpose: Security, error detection, and basic website analytics
  • Duration: Logs are typically deleted after 30-90 days
  • No Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking tools

2.3 Contact Form Data

When you submit a contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message content
  • Subject line

This information is only used to respond to your inquiry. We keep contact submissions for 12 months for record-keeping purposes.

2.4 Cookies & Local Storage

Audio Toolkit may use browser storage for functionality:

  • Essential Cookies: None required (our site works without cookies)
  • Preference Cookies: Optional cookies to remember your settings (tool preferences, theme, language)
  • Local Storage: Browser-based storage for offline functionality
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising

2.5 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your audio file metadata (title, artist, duration—we don't access this)
  • ❌ Personal identification information (unless you voluntarily provide it via contact form)
  • ❌ Behavior tracking (clicks, time spent, mouse movements)
  • ❌ Device identifiers (MAC address, phone IMEI, hardware ID)
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Biometric data
  • ❌ Financial information

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1 Audio Files

Your audio files are used only as follows:

  • ✅ To process them according to your selected tool (trimming, converting, editing)
  • ✅ To generate the output file you download
  • ❌ NOT for training AI or machine learning models
  • ❌ NOT for research purposes without explicit consent
  • ❌ NOT shared with third parties

3.2 Server Logs & Technical Data

  • To diagnose technical issues and errors
  • To protect against abuse, fraud, or security threats
  • To understand basic usage patterns (e.g., which tools are most popular)
  • To improve website performance and reliability

3.3 Contact Form Data

  • To respond to your inquiry or support request
  • To provide customer support
  • To notify you of important updates (only if you opt-in)
  • To improve our services based on feedback

3.4 Legal & Compliance

We may disclose your data if required by law:

  • Court orders or legal subpoenas
  • Government requests (law enforcement)
  • Prevention of fraud or abuse
  • Protection of users' safety

We will challenge any unlawful requests and notify you when legally possible.

4. Data Processing & Storage

4.1 Encryption & Security

  • HTTPS Only: All communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted
  • No Unencrypted Data: Contact form data is encrypted in transit
  • Secure Hosting: Servers are hosted on secure, reputable platforms
  • Access Controls: Only authorized staff can access server logs and contact data

4.2 Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Audio Files (in browser) Until browser closed or refreshed
Server Logs 30-90 days
Contact Form Data 12 months
Browser Cookies/Local Storage Per your browser settings (typically 1 year or until cleared)

4.3 Data Backup

Server logs and contact data may be backed up for business continuity. Backups are:

  • Encrypted and secured
  • Stored securely
  • Deleted after backup retention period expires

4.4 International Data Transfers

Audio Toolkit is hosted in the United States. By using our service, you consent to the transfer of your information to the US, which may have different privacy laws than your home country. We implement safeguards to protect your data during transfer.

5. Your Rights & Choices

5.1 GDPR Rights (EU & UK Residents)

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (with exceptions)
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to certain uses of your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

5.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under CCPA:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected and how it's used
  • Right to delete personal information collected from you
  • Right to opt-out of data selling (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

5.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@audiotoolkit.github.io with:

  • Your request type (access, deletion, portability, etc.)
  • Your email address associated with Audio Toolkit
  • Any relevant details to help us locate your data

We will respond to your request within 30 days (or legally required timeframe).

5.4 Opting Out

  • Email Communications: Unsubscribe by clicking the link in any email
  • Cookies: Clear cookies or adjust browser privacy settings
  • Analytics: We don't use analytics, so there's nothing to opt out of

7. Third-Party Services

7.1 Web Audio API (Browser Feature)

Audio Toolkit uses your browser's built-in Web Audio API for processing. This is a native browser feature, not a third party.

7.2 Speech Recognition (Voice to Text Tool)

The Voice to Text tool uses browser-based speech recognition:

7.5 No Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT share your data with:

  • ❌ Marketing companies

8. Cookies & Tracking

8.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites. They help websites remember your preferences and activities.

8.2 Our Cookie Policy

  • No Required Cookies: Audio Toolkit works perfectly without cookies
  • Optional Preference Cookies: We may use cookies to remember your settings (optional)
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use cookies for tracking, profiling, or advertising

8.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser:

  • Accept or reject cookies when prompted
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Disable cookies entirely (may affect site functionality)
  • Use "Do Not Track" browser feature

8.4 No Tracking Technologies

Audio Toolkit does NOT use:

  • ❌ Google Analytics or similar analytics tools
  • ❌ Heatmap tracking (Hotjar, Inspectlet)
  • ❌ Session recording
  • ❌ Cross-site tracking pixels
  • ❌ Fingerprinting
