The Formats

WebP and PNG: What Is the Difference?

WebP is an image format developed by Google. It can use lossy or lossless compression, supports transparency and can also store animation. It is widely used on the web because files are often smaller than equivalent JPGs or PNGs.PNG is a lossless format with transparency that is accepted by a very wide range of software, which makes it a convenient format for editing and sharing.
What Changes

What Happens When You Convert WebP to PNG

Transparency. It is kept. If the WebP has transparent areas, the PNG does too.Quality. The PNG stores the decoded pixels without further loss. If the WebP was saved in lossy mode, its compression artifacts are already in those pixels and remain.Animation. Animated WebP files are not converted frame by frame. Expect a single still image.File size. The PNG is often larger than the WebP.Metadata. It is normally not carried over, because the PNG is created from the pixels on a canvas.
When To Convert

Why Convert WebP to PNG?

Some editors, document tools and older software do not accept WebP. PNG is a safe choice when you need transparency. If you want a smaller file afterwards, use Compress Image, or turn the PNG into a JPG with PNG to JPG. For a comparison of the formats, see JPG vs PNG vs WebP.

How It Works

How This Converter Works

Your browser opens the WebP file, the tool draws it onto a canvas and downloads the result as PNG. Your browser must be able to display WebP, which current mainstream browsers do. One image is converted at a time, and other formats your browser can open are accepted too.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a transparent background preserved? ▾
Yes. PNG supports transparency, and the tool keeps the alpha channel from the WebP.
Does converting WebP to PNG increase quality? ▾
No. It stores the decoded pixels losslessly but cannot add detail that lossy WebP removed.
What happens to an animated WebP? ▾
Only a still image is produced, not an animation.
Why is my PNG larger than the WebP? ▾
WebP is often more efficient, particularly for photographs. PNG stores pixels without discarding anything.
