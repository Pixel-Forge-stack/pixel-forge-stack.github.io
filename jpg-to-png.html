The Formats

JPG and PNG: What Is the Difference?

JPG (or JPEG) is a lossy format designed for photographs. It keeps files small by discarding some detail, and it cannot store transparency.PNG is a lossless format. It stores the pixels it is given without discarding anything, supports transparency, and handles flat colours, text and sharp edges cleanly. PNG files of photographs are usually much larger than JPGs.
What Changes

What Happens When You Convert JPG to PNG

Quality. The tool decodes the JPG and saves exactly those pixels as PNG. Nothing more is lost in the PNG step, but detail that JPG already discarded does not come back, and existing JPG artifacts remain visible.Transparency. A JPG has none, so the PNG is fully opaque. Converting does not remove a background. For that you need an editor with a background-removal or selection tool.File size. Expect the PNG to be noticeably larger than the JPG, sometimes several times larger for photographs. If size matters, see Compress Image.Metadata. The PNG is created from the pixels on a canvas, so EXIF details such as camera data and location are normally not carried over.
When To Convert

Reasons to Convert, and Reasons Not To

Converting can help when an app or form requires PNG, when you want to edit and re-save without further JPG compression, or when you are going to add transparent elements in a design tool.

It is usually not worth it for ordinary photographs you only want to view or share: the PNG will be larger without looking better. To go the other way, use PNG to JPG; for other conversions see WEBP to PNG. A comparison of all three formats is in JPG vs PNG vs WebP.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality? ▾
No. It prevents further loss from future saves, but it cannot restore detail that the JPG has already lost.
Will the PNG have a transparent background? ▾
No. JPG has no transparency data, so the PNG is opaque. A transparent background has to be created in an image editor.
Why is the PNG so much bigger? ▾
PNG stores every pixel without discarding detail, which suits graphics but is inefficient for photographs.
Is camera or location data kept? ▾
Do not count on it. The conversion redraws the image, and metadata is normally not carried over.
